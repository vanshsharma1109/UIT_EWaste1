Awareness & Education

What is e-waste?

Discarded electrical and electronic equipment: phones, laptops, chargers, batteries, lab and office equipment.

Environmental impact

E-waste can contain lead, mercury, cadmium and other hazardous materials. Burning or dumping it pollutes soil, water and air.

Safe disposal

Do not put e-waste in household bins or sell it to informal dismantlers. Use an authorized recycler through the E-CYCLE team.

Battery safety

Never puncture or crush batteries. If a battery is swollen or hot, stop using it, keep it away from flammable items and tell the team when handing over.

Data wiping before handover

Back up what you need, sign out of accounts, then factory-reset the device. Remove SIM and memory cards.

Reuse and refurbishment

Working devices may be reused or repaired. Say so in your request.
